STANDING ORDERS — HOW TO PRINT ISK WITHOUT BEING THE ASSHOLE
  • Own the gaps first. Anything MISSING sells at whatever you list it for — your margin setting is the only competition. That's the polite monopoly.
  • Restock what dies. The losses table is a pre-order list: hulls with under a month of stock get bought the night after a bad fleet.
  • Match cargo to value. Ark hauls the bulk (hulls, ammo by the million); DSTs carry the dense ISK — implants, drugs, crystals — a full implant restock fits in one DST with room for regret.
  • Undercut the assholes, not yourself. Price just under the gougers and you win every sale while still landing 20-40% over Jita. The main page shows you exactly who to undercut.
  • Watch your own scalp log. If your cheap contracts start getting flipped, raise prices — the market is telling you the fair price is higher.
  • Fuel and minerals are the quiet compounding play. Nobody brags about fuel blocks; the structure burns them regardless of content patches.
